NEXAURA — Next Minds. Bold Innovation

Startup-Friendly Public Procurement Platform 

NEXAURA is a GovTech platform designed to bridge the gap between government departments and innovative startups by enabling government bodies to discover, evaluate, pilot, procure, and scale eligible startup solutions.

🔗 Live Website: [https://www.nexaura.id/](https://www.nexaura.id/)

🚀 Problem Statement

Startup-friendly public procurement mechanism that enables government departments to identify, pilot, procure, and scale innovative solutions from eligible startups.

Traditional government procurement can make it difficult for innovative startups to reach government departments because of:

Limited visibility of relevant startups
Lengthy procurement processes
Difficulty verifying startup credibility
Manual pilot and milestone tracking
Fragmented validation of outcomes
Limited public visibility of pilot progress and fund utilization

💡 Our Solution

NEXAURA provides a centralized digital ecosystem where government departments can post requirements and discover suitable startup solutions.
The platform supports the complete journey:

Discover → Match → Validate → Pilot → Monitor → Procure → Scale

Key Features

🤖 AI-Assisted Startup Matching

Matches government requirements with relevant startups based on factors such as sector, technology, capabilities, and previous pilot experience.

🪪 Digital Pilot Passport

Maintains a structured credibility record containing verified pilot information, outcomes, KPIs, and validation details.

📊 Pilot Monitoring

Tracks pilot progress, milestones, KPIs, and potential risks in one place.

🔐 Role-Based Access Control

Separate workflows and permissions for:
Government Departments
Startup Innovators
Independent Validators
Administrators

💰 Milestone & Payment Tracking

Connects milestone completion with verification and approval workflows to improve accountability.

🛡️ Risk Monitoring

Helps identify potential issues during pilots and provides alerts for timely intervention.

🔎 Independent Validation

Allows independent validators to verify pilot KPIs and reported outcomes.

🌐 Public Transparency Portal

Provides public visibility into selected pilot outcomes, progress, and fund utilization.
